Third-party software in Riddim Forge
====================================

projectM (libprojectM)
  Files: projectM-4.dll (Windows), libprojectM-4.so.4 (Linux), libprojectM-4.4.dylib (macOS),
         next to the Riddim Forge plugin binary and standalone app.
  Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1
           https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
  Source: https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/projectm
          commit 349b7cc2db312d47cef6be24c6856ced6643a385, built unmodified
          (cmake/ProjectM.cmake in the Riddim Machine source shows how).
  Riddim Forge loads this library at run time and works without it. You may replace it with
  your own build of libprojectM 4.2 or newer under the same file name.

  libprojectM includes, under their own permissive licences:
    projectm-eval (MIT), hlslparser (MIT), GLM (MIT), glad (MIT), stb_image (MIT / public domain).

The MilkDrop scenes Riddim Forge shows are original to Riddim Machine (Source/forge/MilkScenes.cpp).
